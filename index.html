Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Sanjwal Model Town provides a mobile application that helps registered customers access their property information, installment details, payment information, documents and support services.

1. Information We Process

The application processes information including customer name, mobile number, CNIC, account details, property records, installment information, payment history and related documents.

Customers may also provide payment receipt images, transaction references, complaints and support messages.

2. How We Use Information

We use customer information to verify eligibility, provide secure account access, display society records, review payment evidence, respond to support requests and maintain relevant transaction records.

3. Account Verification

Account activation is intended for customers whose details match Sanjwal Model Town records. Mobile OTP verification may be provided through an external messaging service.

4. Payment Information

Customers may submit payment receipts for review. Uploading a payment receipt does not automatically mean that a payment has been confirmed or approved.

5. Service Providers

Customer information is processed through backend hosting and database infrastructure used to operate the application. Messaging providers may process mobile numbers when OTP delivery is enabled. Authorized society personnel may access information required to provide customer services.

6. Security

The application uses encrypted HTTPS connections for backend communication and secure device storage for authentication sessions. Access to private customer records is restricted through authentication and authorization controls.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Deleting an app account removes its ability to sign in. App authentication credentials and notifications are removed by the current deletion workflow.

Certain property ownership, financial transactions, payment evidence and dispute-related records may be retained where justified by applicable obligations or legitimate recordkeeping needs.

The retention periods and handling of remaining personal information must be finalized before this policy is used for store submission.

8. Customer Privacy Requests

Customers can request assistance regarding their personal information or account deletion through the contact details provided below.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when our services, providers or information handling practices change.

Terms & Conditions

1. Eligibility

This application is intended for customers registered in Sanjwal Model Town records.

2. Account Security

Customers must keep passwords and OTP codes private. Unauthorized use of another customer's account is prohibited.

3. Property Information

Property and installment details are displayed according to society records. Customers should contact management if information appears incorrect.

4. Payments

Customers should verify official payment instructions. Submitted payment screenshots remain subject to review by authorized personnel.

5. Acceptable Use

Fraudulent payment submissions, impersonation, unauthorized account access and interference with the application are prohibited.

6. Account Deletion

Customers may request deletion of their mobile app account. Deletion of app access does not automatically terminate property ownership, installment agreements or lawful financial obligations.

7. Service Availability

Some services may be temporarily interrupted due to maintenance, network failures or technical problems.

8. Support

Customers can contact Sanjwal Model Town using the email address below.

Request Account Deletion

If you have a Sanjwal Model Town mobile app account, you may request account deletion at any time.

Option 1 — Inside the App

Open the SMT application, sign in, go to Profile, select Delete Account and follow the confirmation instructions.

Option 2 — Without the App

Email our support team with the subject "SMT App Account Deletion Request". You do not need to reinstall the app.

Request Account Deletion

Identity Verification

Our support team may ask for appropriate verification before processing a deletion request.

Do not send your account password, OTP, full CNIC or payment receipts in the initial email.

Information Removed or Retained

The current in-app deletion process removes login credentials and app notifications. Some resolved support records are also removed.

Property, financial, payment verification and certain dispute records may remain where retention is justified. Requests concerning other personal information will require review of the applicable retention obligations.

You may also request information about the personal data retained following account deletion.
